Legal, in plain English

Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026. This explains what All Friction collects about you, why, and how you can see, fix or delete it.

Who we are

All Friction is a free NCEA study resource run by Jayden Buntting in New Zealand. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean All Friction. You can contact us at jaydenbuntting@gmail.com.

You can use the lessons without an account. You only need an account if you want to save your progress.

What we collect

When you make an account we collect:

  • Your name and email address, from Google, Microsoft, or the email you type in.
  • Your school, picked from a list of New Zealand schools.
  • Your year level (Year 9 to 13).
  • Your age group (under 13, 13 to 15, 16 to 17, or 18 or over). We ask for a group, not your birthday.
  • The subjects and levels you choose on your dashboard.
  • Which lesson slides you have viewed, and when, so we can show your progress and suggest what to study next.
  • The time you agreed to this policy.

We never see or store your Google or Microsoft password.

Why we collect it

  • To let you sign in and keep your account.
  • To save your progress and recommend what to study next.
  • To understand, in summary, which schools, year levels and subjects use the site, so we know what to build next.
  • To contact you about your account if we really need to.

We do not sell your information. We do not show ads. We do not share your details with other students, schools or advertisers.

Who can see it

Only you (when logged in) and the person running All Friction. Other members cannot see your details or progress. We may look at information across all members in a summarised way, for example how many students from each year level use a subject.

Where it is stored

Your account information is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in a data centre in Sydney, Australia. That means your information is stored outside New Zealand. Sign-in is handled by Google or Microsoft if you choose those options.

Some things stay only on your own device and never reach us, for example your answers in the online past papers, which are saved in your browser. Your login session is also kept in your browser so you stay signed in.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when a page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you delete your account, your details, subjects and progress are permanently deleted.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can:

  • See the information we hold about you. Your dashboard shows most of it, or email us for the rest.
  • Correct it. Use "Edit my details" on your dashboard.
  • Delete it. Use "Delete my account" on your dashboard. This is permanent and takes effect straight away.

If you think we have handled your information badly, please email us first so we can fix it. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

If you are under 16

You are welcome to use All Friction. If you are under 16, we encourage you to talk to a parent or caregiver before you make an account, so they know what you are signing up for. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or caregiver to help you.

Keeping it safe

Accounts are protected by Google, Microsoft or a one-time email link, and our database is set up so each member can only read their own information. No system is perfect. If we learn of a privacy breach that could cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Privacy Commissioner as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top and, where we can, let members know.
